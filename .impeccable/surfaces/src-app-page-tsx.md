# Landing

Mode: Persuade
Audience: advisors first; private banks, family offices, and carriers second
Action: Connect with Yuvarra / Start the Conversation

## Direction contract

THESIS: One private-banking room that holds. The first viewport pins; Hong Kong, Cayman, and the United States dock as a seal; the platform is turned like pages in that same room. It refuses a stacked product landing and it refuses cinematic overlay, teal, or agency scroll cues.

OWN-WORLD: Cool stone, ink, sage from the logo, hairline rules, Schibsted Grotesk display, Source Sans 3. Monument type on paper, photography as architecture, no new color or face.

STORY: An advisor reads the locked offer, watches the three centres take the room, then reads the platform without leaving it, and starts a conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Below the existing chrome, a remaining-viewport split: locked headline, subhead, and Connect on paper at left (seven columns); harbour plate as a tall architectural field at right (five). No kicker. Primary action visible without scroll. As the visitor moves, an ink blotter rises from the bottom of that same frame with the three centres, then the locked platform copy, one capability at a time. Reduced motion and small screens render the same copy as a static house page.

FORM: Scroll theater, quiet-bank register. User-locked 2026-09-20: scroll_theater + bank. No concept seed; request was specified.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: pin the first room; dock the jurisdictions; page the four capabilities with scroll, with click still working.
